Advanced Hybrid CNN-ViT Ensemble with Attention and FPN for Retinal OCT Disease Classification

Venue: IDAA 2025, Daffodil International University

Retinal diseases can be considered one of the leading causes of vision loss on the global scene, and OCT imaging is crucial in the timely diagnosis of the disease. In this paper, a hybrid model of deep learning, which combines Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs), Vision Transformers (ViTs), Feature Pyramid Networks (FPN) and cross-modal attention, is proposed to simultaneously exploit local retinal texture and global structural features. An ensemble mechanism is used to combine three fusion strategies into attention-based, concatenation strategy, and weighted fusion to enhance robustness and accuracy. Experiments on a publicly available OCTDL dataset of seven disease classes get 96.3% accuracy and a 95.1% macro F1-score and outperform the traditional CNN and ViT baselines. These results prove the high promise of the hybrid ensemble models towards the effective retinal OCT multi-disease classification.

Semi-Supervised Custom Attention UNet for Automated Knee Joint Segmentation in X-Ray Images

Venue: Final Year Defense Project, Daffodil International University

Knee joint segmentation is necessary in precise analysis of knee X-ray images as it allows clinicians to identify structural changes and judge osteoarthritis with more reliability and consistency. Manual interpretation, even though commonly used, is commonly time consuming and likely to be variably affected and thus the creation of an automated and consistent segmentation technique becomes extremely useful in clinical practice. This paper presents a Semi-Supervised Custom Attention U-Net that will provide accurate knee joint segmentation in the various degrees of osteoarthritis. The study applies two publicly available datasets on the IEEE DataPort and Mendeley Data, where 250 images, which are equally representative of all of the KL grades, were selected. The images were downsampled and optimized with Adaptive Gamma-CLAHE and manually labelled masks were generated with the guidance of a trained medical professional to aid the supervised training phase. The suggested model combines an EfficientNet-B4 encoder and a Custom Attention decoder that enables it to adopt high-resolution anatomical edges and preserve a powerful features localization. Moreover, the model uses a semi-supervised learning scheme, which integrates labeled and unlabeled images, using consistency regularization and pseudo-labeling, and progressive ramp up strategy that together enhance learning stability and decrease dependency on large annotated databases. Experimental findings show that the model has a uniform high segmentation accuracy with a Dice score of 0.94 in low grades of KL and 0.90 in high cases and IoU score of 0.87-0.80. The solution is both scalable and precise, as it is able to segment knee joints and can be useful in detecting osteoarthritis, as well as long-term monitoring of diseases.
